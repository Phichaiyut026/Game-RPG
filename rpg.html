  • Receive quarantine sweep operations from Commander Vance and mutant zombie bounties from Drill Sgt. Miller. Look for ! / ? indicators.
  • Zombies mutate and grow stronger further east. Sell biohazard samples to Quartermaster Higgins, buy medkits, and customize firearms at his gunsmith anvil.
  • Each rank grants 2 specialization points to invest in combat skills or tactical training tree.
  • Bunker B3 & Rifts: stairs at ruined city center lead down to Bunker B3 to neutralize the Nemesis Tyrant bio-weapon. Step onto the Abyssal Jump Pad for endless challenges!
  • Armor and energy recover rapidly inside Quarantine Outpost Alpha base perimeter.
